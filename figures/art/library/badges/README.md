# The badges of ניהול מטרות – embroidered patches

Made with the design image tool (Gemini) on 2026-11-13, with two cushions as the style references (`/assets/pillows/p05.webp`, `p13.webp`), square, one per call, at about $0.068 each. Cut from white with `figures/art/cut-white.py`: 640 px here, 320 px in `public/assets/badges/`. The fabric tells the shelf: charcoal for consistency (fire), forest green for progress (the way), sand for family, midnight blue and gold for the rare ones.

## The prompt (one template; the fabric and the motif change)

```
A round embroidered patch badge, drawn in exactly the same illustrated style as the reference cushions: a confident hand-drawn dark ink outline, flat rich colours, and Palestinian / Middle-Eastern cross-stitch (tatreez) embroidery made of visible little X stitches. The patch is a circle with a thick raised merrowed border of twisted thread all around its edge, and just inside it a thin ring of small geometric tatreez pattern. The fabric is deep charcoal-black wool fabric, the threads in ember red, flame orange and warm cream. In the centre, a bold, simple, clearly readable cross-stitch motif of a campfire of crossed logs with three tall curling flames and a few sparks. Front view, flat, with a subtle fabric texture and a soft shadow under the patch. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere. The single patch is centred on a plain pure white background and fills about 86% of the square frame.```
