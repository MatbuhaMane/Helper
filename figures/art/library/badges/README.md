# The badges of ניהול מטרות – embroidered patches

Made with the design image tool (Gemini) on 2026-11-13, with two cushions as the style references (`/assets/pillows/p05.webp`, `p13.webp`), square, one per call, at about $0.068 each. Cut from white with `figures/art/cut-white.py`: 640 px here, 320 px in `public/assets/badges/`. The fabric tells the shelf: charcoal for consistency (fire), forest green for progress (the way), sand for family, midnight blue and gold for the rare ones.

## The prompt (one template; the fabric and the motif change)

```
A round embroidered patch badge, drawn in exactly the same illustrated style as the reference cushions: a confident hand-drawn dark ink outline, flat rich colours, and Palestinian / Middle-Eastern cross-stitch (tatreez) embroidery made of visible little X stitches. The patch is a circle with a thick raised merrowed border of twisted thread all around its edge, and just inside it a thin ring of small geometric tatreez pattern. The fabric is deep charcoal-black wool fabric, the threads in ember red, flame orange and warm cream. In the centre, a bold, simple, clearly readable cross-stitch motif of a campfire of crossed logs with three tall curling flames and a few sparks. Front view, flat, with a subtle fabric texture and a soft shadow under the patch. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere. The single patch is centred on a plain pure white background and fills about 86% of the square frame.```

## Each one's motif

- **peg** (fire): a single wooden tent peg driven into the ground with a looped rope tied to it, a tiny green sprout beside it
- **ember** (fire): one small glowing ember coal with a tiny curling flame rising from it
- **fire** (fire): a campfire of crossed logs with three tall curling flames and a few sparks
- **eternal** (fire): a small square stone altar with a steady tall flame burning on top, never going out
- **rest_return** (fire): two lit Shabbat candles in simple candlesticks, side by side, soft glow around the flames
- **rest_seven** (fire): two lit Shabbat candlesticks in the centre surrounded by a ring of seven small eight-pointed stars
- **pole** (path): a single tall wooden tent pole standing upright with two taut ropes pegged to the ground
- **standing** (path), redrawn on 2026-11-13 at the owner's word, with the tent's picture (`/assets/tent.webp`) as a second reference: a wide, low black Bedouin goat-hair tent like ours, its flat roof gently sagging between several poles (not a triangle), its front open on a woven striped interior, ropes out to pegs on both sides.
- **journey** (path): a winding trail of small footprints climbing over gentle hills toward a little star
- **tracker** (path): a magnifying glass over a line of footprints in the sand, a tracker reading the trail
- **dawn** (path), redrawn on 2026-11-13 at the owner's word: a half sun with straight rays rising from behind a clear horizon line of two wide, low, rounded desert hills (plainly hills of earth and sand, nothing like leaves), the morning star still above. *Edited by hand:* the model stitched the star as a cross; it was covered with the patch's own fabric and re-stitched as an eight-pointed star of gold cross-stitches.
- **night** (path): a crescent moon and stars above a small glowing oil lantern
- **traveller** (path): a camel walking over dunes with a small travel bundle on its back, a long road behind it
- **gifts** (kin): three small tied cloth gift pouches with ribbons, one larger in the middle
- **milestones** (path): a stacked cairn of five rounded stones beside a path
- **highway** (path): a straight road running to the horizon with small milestone stones on its sides
- **way_of_life** (path): a winding road leading to a tree with full leaves and a few pomegranates
- **the_way** (secret): a radiant tree of life with deep roots and spreading branches, a road winding through its roots, small stars around it
- **kings_way** (secret): a crown hovering above a long ancient road paved with stones that stretches to the horizon
- **tribe** (kin): four small tents standing together in a circle around a little fire
- **angel** (kin): a pair of embroidered wings around a small glowing lantern lighting a path
- **adviser** (kin): an ancient clay oil lamp with a small bright flame and a tiny heart above it
- **shoulder** (kin): two hands clasped firmly together in a handshake of support
