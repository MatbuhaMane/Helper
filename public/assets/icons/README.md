# The app icon: a small tent (vision batch 8, 2026-11-14)

The ordinary icon of האוהל is our tent (from `figures/art/library/home/tent.webp`) on limestone, with a patch of sand under it.
The family logo (`/icon.svg`, `/icon-*.png`) stays the owner's personal icon; the master serves it per user.

- `tent-icon-{512,192,180,48,32}.png` – "any": rounded square, transparent corners.
- `tent-icon-maskable-{512,192}.png` – "maskable": full bleed, the tent inside the safe circle (66% wide).
- `apple-touch-icon.png` (180) – full bleed; iOS rounds it.

At 48 and 32 px the detailed drawing turns into a brown blot that still reads as a tent; a simplified small-tent drawing for those sizes is
planned with the next image quota.
